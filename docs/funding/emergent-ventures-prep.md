## The program (verified Oct 2026)

- Apply: <https://www.mercatus.org/emergent-ventures> → form at
  mercatus.tfaforms.net.
- One 1,500-word proposal that must stand alone; supplementary
  attachments allowed (no PDFs).
- Individuals 13+, worldwide. Typical turnaround ~1 week.
- No published award size; both small and large grants made.
- They fund *the person and the idea's trajectory* — open with your
  personal story, not the tech. Credentials matter less than why you.
- Signature question: **"What is one mainstream or 'consensus' view
  that you absolutely agree with?"** — their calibration test; answer
  genuinely, not strategically.
- "Profit is not a problem — a quick path to revenue self-sufficiency
  is a feature, not a bug."

## Angle that fits EV's taste

EV likes zero-to-one bets with weirdness and scale. Honest framings
that fit:

- **"Your laptop is an unmonetized datacenter"** — the contrarian part
  is the *no-install* supply side and mutual-credit settlement instead
  of a token.
- **A commons that sells** — demand APIs monetize community-owned
  capacity; the self-sufficiency line is already wired (demand desk,
  payer ledger, receivables).
- **Mesh reach** — machine resources for people off the grid; nobody
  else is doing demand-side mesh.
