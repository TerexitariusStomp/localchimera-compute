# Fiscal host decision memo — OSC vs SFC vs SPI

> **Why this exists:** several institutional lanes require an
> organizational applicant (NSF PESOSE, ARIN, ISOC, Sloan-class). A
> fiscal host gives the project a nonprofit umbrella without
> incorporating — donations become tax-deductible, grant checks can be
> made out to the host on the project's behalf, and the "entity"
> question is deferred until/unless the commercial spin-out happens.

## Comparison

| | **Open Source Collective** | **Software Freedom Conservancy** | **SPI** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type | 501(c)(6) fiscal host | 501(c)(3) member project | 501(c)(3) associated project |
| Fee | 10% of funds raised | ~10% (negotiated) | 5% typical |
| Speed | Days–weeks, form-based | Months, deliberative by design | Weeks–months, board vote |
| Online tools | Full Open Collective platform (transparent ledger) | Minimal — they hold funds, you request spends | Basic donation pages |
| Selectivity | Moderate (verify legitimacy, license, governance) | High — copyleft-friendly projects favored (AGPL is a plus) | Moderate — Debian-associated culture |
| Grant receipts | Can receive on your behalf | Yes — many institutional funders recognize SFC | Yes |
| Fit for us | Strongest operational fit: donation rails, transparent books, fast | Best long-term legitimacy; slowest | Middle ground |

## Recommendation

**Apply to OSC now (done — parked at the login wall), and send the SFC
inquiry email in parallel.** OSC is the fast operational rail
(donations, transparent budget, GitHub Sponsors payouts can route
through it). SFC is the legitimacy play — Conservancy membership is a
strong signal for the institutional grant lanes and costs nothing to
inquire. They are not exclusive; a project can hold both
relationships.

## What a host unlocks

- **NSF PESOSE** (Mar 2, 2027; $300k Phase I) — needs nonprofit or
  for-profit applicant; OSC can be listed as the applicant/fiscal agent
  in many cases, or the application goes through an IHE partner.
- **ARIN Community Grant** (spring 2027 window) — organizational
  applicants preferred.
- **Tax-deductible donations** — Drips/Giveth-style rails plus
  OpenCollective's own recurring-donation page.
- **Expense transparency** — the collective ledger doubles as the
  public budget the funding pack already promises.
