under the floor

yours to run, all the way down

Every layer is free software you could run yourself — the same package graph on a laptop, on a mesh radio, and on the box in production.

ValueFlows

economics
The vocabulary the planner solves over — needs, offers, processes and commitments as one grammar. The interface, the rules and the kernel all speak it, which is why there is only one of it. valueflo.ws ↗

KERI

identity & data
Self-sovereign identity, and the data that travels with it. You hold your own keys and your own event log; your credentials and records stay portable, and you disclose exactly the parts you choose. keri.one ↗

Reticulum

mesh transport
Carries capabilities over whatever wire is actually there — Tor, LoRa, packet radio, plain TCP — and treats every one of them as a real network. reticulum.network ↗

Goblins

actors
Spritely's distributed object-capability actor model. Governance, matching and the planner itself are vats of Goblins actors talking to peers over OCapN. spritely.institute ↗

GUIX

kernel
Reproducible builds and declarative deployment — the ground every other layer reconfigures onto. guix.gnu.org ↗
